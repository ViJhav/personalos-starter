# Inbox

Dump anything here. Raw thoughts, errands, links, things to buy, things to look into, half-formed ideas, something someone mentioned that you do not want to lose.

**This file is allowed to be messy.** That is its job. It exists so nothing else has to be.

No formatting rules. No categories. One line each, or a paragraph, or a pasted link with no explanation. Do not tidy it, do not sort it, do not feel bad about its length.

Triage it when it gets full or during the weekly review: run `/prompt inbox-triage`. Each item ends up in one of five places: a next action on the weekly plan, an update to a project or area file, a logged decision, a reference note, or the bin. Most things are the bin, and that is a good outcome.
