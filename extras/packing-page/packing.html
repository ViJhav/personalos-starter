Paste your packing list below: one item per line, with ## Headings for each place in the house (bathroom, closet, desk). Then hit Save. Editing later keeps every item's status, as long as you don't reword it.

Format and tags 
# Trip name             ← the page title
First line under it     ← shown under the title

## Bathroom             ← a category, one stop around the house
- Toothbrush
- Sunscreen {order}     ← adds an "Ordered" step: buy it first
- Contacts {decide: wearing them this trip?}
- Swimsuit {after: laundry}   ← waits until you mark "laundry" done
- Phone charger {day-of}      ← waits for the day you leave
- [x] Passport          ← starts as packed

## Before leaving {todo}  ← a to-do section: tap = done
- Water plants
- Hold the mail
