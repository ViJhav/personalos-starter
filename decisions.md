# Decisions

A log of decisions worth not re-thinking. Newest at the top.

The value is almost entirely in the **reasoning** and the **tradeoffs**. Six months from now the thing you will have forgotten is not what you chose, it is why you ruled out the other option, and without that you will re-open the whole question from scratch.

Worth logging: anything you spent real time deciding, anything you might second-guess later, anything where you deliberately chose the worse-looking option for a reason. Also worth logging: decisions to *not* do something, which are the easiest to forget having made.

Not worth logging: routine choices you will never revisit.

## YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>

- **Decision:** what you chose
- **Reasoning:** why, including what you were optimizing for
- **Tradeoffs:** what you gave up, and what would make you revisit this
- **Follow-up:** anything this obligates, if applicable
